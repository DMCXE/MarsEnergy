# Proven Reference Showcase: MarsEnergy 11-Asset Kit

This showcase documents concrete phrasing distilled from the **MarsEnergy (Utopia Planitia)** 11-asset production batch, where two sequential generation rounds (8 core assets + 3 modular standard assets) maintained seamless visual, material, and camera continuity across `.glb` models and `2400 × 1600` RGBA `.png` renders.

---

## 1. Locked-Camera Cutaway Pair (Physical Cutaway + A/B State Lock)
- **Use case**: Comparing full-power (`25 kWe / 12h`) vs. preservation-mode (`15 kWe / 8h`) pressurized greenhouse lighting.
- **Key prompt techniques**:
  - Kept 6 off-white structural ribs intact across the top while slicing `120°` open on the front shell so wall thickness and interior 3-tier racks read clearly.
  - Locked identical camera and plinth coordinates; changed only active overhead LED bar count and interior illumination.
  - **Domain guardrail**: Explicitly required pressurized arched shell + end airlock service box (`no Earth open-air plastic greenhouse`).

## 2. Proportional Cutaway Interior (Data-Mapped 3:7 Spatial Split)
- **Use case**: Visualizing `3.6 MWh` 48-hour emergency backup (`1.0 MWh` battery + `2.6 MWh` PEM fuel cell).
- **Key prompt techniques**:
  - Explicitly allocated `left 30%` to modular drawer-style Li-ion racks with cyan indicator bars and `right 70%` to vertical PEM cabinets + twin composite tanks (green band larger H₂ tank, blue band smaller O₂ tank).
  - Visually isolated the dedicated green vital power bus from the disconnected external grid breaker.
  - **Domain guardrail**: Isolated strictly to habitat vital backup (`no rocket propellant plumbing or water plant lines`).

## 3. Cross-Asset Shared Modular Interface (Exploded Hot-Swap + 1U CubeSat)
- **Use case**: Visually proving that a ground microgrid bus board (`P0-A`) and a `100 × 100 × 100 mm` CubeSat 1U power payload (`P0-B`) share the exact same standardized connector.
- **Key prompt techniques**:
  - **P0-A (Bus Board)**: Parallel emerald vital rail + thin cyan telemetry rail + amber dispatchable rail feeding 4 identical keyed slots; first 3 cubes plugged flush, 4th amber cube lifted **`15 mm`** vertically in a hot-swap pose exposing **8 gold pogo-pins and 2 asymmetric alignment keys**.
  - **P0-B (1U CubeSat)**: Exact `1:1:1` anodized aluminum 4-rail skeleton with open front/right panels exposing 3-tier PC/104 stacking (bottom TEG + dark-grey finned heatsink + backup cells; middle teal PCB with gold pin headers; top 45°-deployed dark-blue solar wing + folded antenna), specifying **identical underside 8-pin keyed connector geometry to P0-A**.

## 4. Geological Cylinder Cross-Section Pair (Matched Horizon & Strata Guardrail)
- **Use case**: Contrasting Utopia Planitia subsurface ice melt-well vs. Gale Crater hydrated clay kiln.
- **Key prompt techniques**:
  - Standardized cylindrical terrain plinths sliced open `110°` at the front with identical scale and horizon.
  - **Utopia**: Thick reddish-brown dry regolith cleanly above pale icy-blue subsurface ice stratum + insulated coaxial melt-well pipe + bell-shaped melt cavity + amber waste-heat inlet. **Guardrail**: `ice strictly buried under red regolith — no open surface ice lake`.
  - **Gale**: Layered dark-brown/slate-grey sedimentary rock and clay + excavator scoop → screw auger → copper-orange rotary thermal kiln → condenser → tank + dry tailings pile. **Guardrail**: `no blue ice layer anywhere`.

## 5. Safe Low-Voltage Desktop Rig & Minimalist Scale Diorama
- **Desktop Kits (`P0-2a / P0-2b`)**: Matte dark-grey anti-static lab baseplate, physical toggle switches, blank dark OLED screens (zero gibberish characters), swappable multi-fin vertical heatsink vs. flat aluminum comparison plate placed neatly beside the enclosed orange-guarded heater block. **Guardrail**: `safe low-voltage DC educational apparatus — no open flame, no 220V wall plugs`.
- **Campus Priority Diorama (`P2-5`)**: 6 clean geometric campus buildings on a chamfered matte plinth linked by slim colored cable trenches — 3 mint-cyan lit vital buildings, 2 soft-blue dimmed classrooms, 1 completely darkened sports hall. **Guardrail**: `minimalist architectural massing — no random trees, no realistic pedestrians`.
